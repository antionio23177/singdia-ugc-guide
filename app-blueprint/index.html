Every Create button shows the cost

“Create video · ? coins” with duration and output size immediately above it. Face replacement, cinematic stories and songs each have their own visible quote.

₹ per output = pack price ÷ usable pack coins × output coin cost. This is an estimate tied to the selected pack, not a universal exchange rate.

Failure should not cost a second time

Proposed rule: reserve coins once; settle only on delivery; release them on technical failure. Show the transaction in Profile. A user-requested new version is a new paid generation, with its cost shown first.

Billing route, taxes, pack expiry, refunds and native app checkout require owner/developer decisions before launch. This page takes no payments.

The India launch approach

01

Lead with an occasion

Start with birthday, wedding, anniversary and personal hero moments. Use an occasion name and a sample result, rather than a model name.

02

Make language feel local

Propose English, Hindi and Hinglish for first-release prompts and song choices. Review Hindi copy before launch. Add regional languages after quality checks.

03

Make the first result shareable

Default to a short vertical clip. Offer Save and the device share sheet; let the person choose WhatsApp, Instagram or another destination.

04

Validate a small collection

Launch-test the five marked “Start here” concepts. Compare selection, paid completion, successful delivery and user-initiated sharing. Expand winners across the 20 concepts.

A MODERATE FIRST RELEASE

Small enough to build.
Complete enough to use.

One fixed recipe per template. The product chooses the provider and settings behind the scenes. Cap complex cases instead of exposing a studio editor.

BUILD FIRST

The essential experience

  • Home with Song and AI Video cards
  • Template gallery, categories and favourites
  • One photo + preset motion, or a short idea
  • Single-person face replacement in approved short scenes
  • One-scene cinematic story with one subject
  • Song story form, lyric review and name-pronunciation hint
  • One account, coin balance and creation library
  • Processing, failure recovery, Save and device sharing
LIMIT THE COMPLEXITY

Useful, with boundaries

  • Proposed video default: 5 seconds, 9:16, 720p; verify provider support
  • Clip input: fixed length, one visible subject, one face
  • Face swap: own or permitted face; no celebrity default scenes
  • Music attachment: existing owned/licensed song, optional
  • Couple scenes and motion transfer require quality checks
  • Show only the upload fields the selected template needs
  • No exact generation-time guarantee before measurement
  • Pause off-screen previews and respect reduced motion
LATER

Leave room to grow

  • Multi-scene films and character training
  • Full music-video agent or long lip-sync performance
  • Timeline, layers, manual masking and motion brushes
  • Hundreds of selectable models
  • Multi-person swaps and unrestricted clip editing
  • 4K, long videos and batch generation
  • Public creator feed and automatic social posting
  • Recurring subscriptions before repeat-use demand is known
Builder handoff: states, defaults and open decisions

Input → output contract

Templates define input type, people count, duration, aspect ratio, output size and cost before submission. Photo, clip and prompt paths converge on the same job screen and result page. The provider must pass quality checks for each recipe.

Keep the draft intact

Sign-in, payment failure, back navigation and network loss must retain the selected template and fields. Users can cancel the account or plan sheet and return to their draft. Only a confirmed Create action starts a paid job.

Media privacy

Use private storage and expiring media links. Let users delete source media and creations. Show face-use permission at the relevant upload. Pick a retention period and vendors before launch; this prototype uploads nothing.

Required states

Empty input · invalid file · missing face · missing permission · signed out · insufficient coins · payment pending/failed · queued · generating · completed · technically failed · offline · deleted.

What needs a decision?

Provider and recipe quality? Native billing? ₹ pack prices and coins? Coin expiry? Usage licence? Video retention? Exact song outputs? Refund rules? All remain open; no revenue projections or private business numbers are included here.

Acceptance checks

A returning creator with coins can pick a template, provide its inputs and press Create. First-time authentication/payment does not erase the draft. Every result returns to My creations. Failed jobs release reserved coins once.
