3 · Your story — the most important part

Tell the bot real, specific details in your message or voice note. Use the examples below as inspiration: names, habits, memories, inside jokes and the message you want the song to carry.

A · Their name

Use the name you call them. Check it in the lyrics and listen for its pronunciation before filming.

e.g.Priya · Maa · Rajesh bhai
B · What they do
1She's a nurse at Apollo, works night shifts, and still wakes me up with chai in the morning.
2Papa drove an auto for 20 years so I could finish school. He never took a single day off.
C · How they look or feel
1Always calm, never raises her voice, and somehow she's always right.
2Big moustache, strict face, and the softest heart in the whole house.
D · Memories or inside jokes 👌 (the magic one)

These details make the song personal. Share actual moments — a place, a date, a habit or a joke — and check how they appear in the lyrics.

1The night our car broke down near Lonavala and we ate vada pav in the rain — she still calls it our best date.
2Every Sunday she steals the last piece of biryani off my plate and swears she didn't touch it.
3She's called me “Motu” since our first year in college and still can't say it without laughing.
E · What this song should say

The message you never say out loud. Write it like you're saying it to their face.

1Tell her that after four years I still get nervous before I call her — and that I'd choose this life with her all over again.
2Tell Maa that every rupee she saved for my school made me who I am, and that I finally want to say thank you out loud.
Free notes

Anything else — dates, names, places, little facts. Nothing here is too small.

e.g.We got married on 12 March 2021 in Jaipur. Her mother's name is Sunita. We have a dog called Simba who only listens to her. She says she hates surprises — this is my first real one in four years.

4 · What to film

Raw video: from 3 minutes. Keep the camera rolling through all three parts:

  • Before — the setup, the nerves, a live chat with them
  • The full song — play it from start to end, don't cut it short
  • After — a short talk once it ends: what they felt, what they say

The peak we need: the moment they hear their own name and react — tears, laugh, hug, a quiet “thank you”.

5 · Filming & sound

✅ DO

  • Place the phone, then sit beside them in the frame
  • Play the full song from start to finish on an external speaker.
  • Let it happen naturally — don't coach them
  • Keep faces clear and well lit

❌ DON'T

  • Don't use the phone speaker, earphones or headphones for the surprise.
  • Don't film in a noisy place
  • Don't point the camera straight at their face the whole time
  • Don't shoot horizontal

6 · Footage checklist

✅ Ready for review

  • Vertical 9:16, Full HD 1080 × 1920 or higher, clear and well lit
  • Raw, 3+ minutes, unedited
  • Real, visible emotion
  • Song clearly audible from a speaker

❌ Rejected

  • Horizontal, blurry or dark
  • Song not audible / noisy background
  • Staged, lifeless reaction
  • Shorter than 3 minutes or already edited
